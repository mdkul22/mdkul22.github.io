The Anarchy goes into detail for the initial rise of the EIC and how it entered the Indian system. What the book lacked is a lack of research in the Maratha and Mysore period more specifically the inner workings of the Maratha confederacy between 1700-1800. Even the Anglo-Maratha wars weren't talked about in detail and mostly glossed over. The rise of the Sikh empire and its allied assistance to the British alongside its rivalry with the Afghans is not mentioned at all. Given that it happened between 1800-1850 at least the first half could have been better contextualized. 

Other than that there seems to be the author assuming the rulers during that time are mostly areligious and using religion mostly for maintaining control. The author assumes rulers in that time had a relative disassociation from their religions, especially Hindus.
